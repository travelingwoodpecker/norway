July 6–23, 2018 · Motorcycle tour

Norway & Sweden

Fifteen days over the Arctic Circle and back: north from Oslo through Lillehammer and Trondheim, up the Helgeland coast to Lofoten and Narvik, across to Swedish Lapland at Kiruna and Jokkmokk, down the Gulf of Bothnia, and home over the mountains by Östersund and Røros.
