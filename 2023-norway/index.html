July 9–19, 2023 · Ten-day motorcycle tour

Norway: Fjords & Scenic Routes

A 1,150-mile loop from Stavanger through Western Norway’s fjord country, riding seven of the National Scenic Routes, from Ryfylke and Hardanger to Geiranger–Trollstigen and Sognefjellet, with a day off the bikes for the train and a hike at Finse.

Pick up the bikes at 9 am, then straight into Ryfast, the world’s longest and deepest undersea road tunnel, about 8 miles and 13 minutes to Tau. The Ryfylke Scenic Route carries on north; on the E134, turn onto Røldalsfjellet.

Hardanger House is a family-owned design hotel of just nine rooms, each with fjord views, between the Hardangerfjord and Folgefonna National Park. Dinner is a set three-course menu at 7 pm.

The road

Gamle Strynefjellsvegen

The old road between East and West Norway is a museum piece in itself. Built by hand at the end of the 19th century, it is lined with meticulously cut stone walls and long rows of guard stones, and has been a protected road since 2009.

Day 7 · Sun, Jul 16

Finse — a day off the bikes

Leave the bikes at the hotel, check the bags, pack lunch boxes and take the 8:22 am Flåm Railway up to Myrdal, then change to the Bergen Railway for Finse.

The hike: an easy hour and a half from Finse to Appelsinhytta, then about two hours up through Danskeskaret to the summit. It is steep for the first ten minutes or so, then crosses old snowfields (not glacier) with a close look at the Blue Ice.

Day 8 · Mon, Jul 17

Flåm Voss

40 mi · about 1 hr

Bicycle down the Rallarvegen to Flåm in the morning, then a short ride to Voss.

Store Ringheim is a farm hotel: old farm buildings around a courtyard, in the same family since 1778, with six rooms (the most historic in a building over 300 years old) and a restaurant cooking from scratch with local ingredients.
